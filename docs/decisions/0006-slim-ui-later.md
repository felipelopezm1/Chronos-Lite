# 0006: A slim UI, later

**Date:** 2026-11-17. **Status:** accepted.

## The choice

Lite ships with no UI at first. A slim one (the queue, a quest's events, its report) is stage 6 and is written fresh, not cut down from Chronos's face, loom and record views.

## Why

The callers are Hephaestus and curl. A UI cut down from the full one would carry its whole contract.
