# 0017: The licence's copyright line names the Chronos Lite Authors

**Date:** 2026-11-17. **Status:** proposed.

## The choice

The LICENSE copied from Hephaestus Lite carried a person's name in its copyright line. In Lite it reads "Copyright 2026 The Chronos Lite Authors", and the edition check keeps the owner's name out of every file.

## Why

Nothing personal ships, and a white-label edition goes out under other companies' names. The owner may restore a named holder; then the edition check needs an exception for that one line.
