# 0004: Three brains

**Date:** 2026-11-17. **Status:** accepted.

## The choice

`forge/brains.json` holds three brains, taken from Chronos's catalogue with their files and sha256: Qwen3.5-0.8B (the Offspring, on the CPU), Qwen3.5-4B (the Promethean, and the brain a size s quest leads with) and Qwen3.5-9B (the Titan of a size m quest: it plans and judges). Their llama-server ports are 7710, 7720 and 7730.

## Why

These are the brains of Chronos's s and m tiers; nothing else is needed for the two sizes Lite keeps. The ports are a hundred above Chronos's own so the two never collide.
